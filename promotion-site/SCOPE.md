## Revisions, payment and delivery

- Each revision round is one consolidated list: up to 10 minor in-scope changes
  or 60 minutes of implementation, whichever comes first. Delivery mistakes do
  not count against rounds. Request feedback within five business days.
- Collect 50% to begin; schedule after complete required materials and payment.
  Final balance is due within seven calendar days of written approval and before
  launch. Silence is not approval. Agree on the project schedule individually.
- Missing content/feedback for ten business days can pause the schedule after
  notice. Sixty calendar days of inactivity can lead to archive after notice;
  resuming requires schedule/scope review, with no automatic restart fee.
- Report agreed-delivery defects within 30 calendar days after launch. This is a
  reporting window, not a repair deadline. Acknowledge within two business days
  and give a plan after reproduction. Correct provider delivery mistakes without
  charge or use of care/revision allowances, including clients without care.

## One optional care plan

**Website Care — $59/month for either package**, explicitly selected and billed
in advance on the launch/enrollment anniversary. No required twelve-month term.

- One monthly health check, normally in the first five business days after
  renewal, **separate from request time**: key pages/navigation, visible form
  behavior, dependency notices and obvious loading/deployment problems, with a
  short completion note. No continuous monitoring or comprehensive security audit.
- **30 combined minutes per billing month** for existing-content edits, minor
  fixes and technical help. No rollover, transfers or allowance credits. Count
  actual hands-on request work; routine billing questions, acknowledgment and
  corrections to provider delivery mistakes do not use the allowance.
- Compatible small patches/fixes can use request time; major upgrades, new pages,
  redesigns, account recovery, complex provider incidents and extensive recovery
  need a separately approved quote. No automatic overage billing.
- Email request processing: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM U.S. Central Time, excluding
  U.S. federal holidays. Acknowledge within two business days; routine edits target
  five business days after complete instructions/access. External dependencies
  may affect completion. No staffed live desk or 24/7 support.
- Only authorized real form tests: after form changes and quarterly if authorized.
  Provider acceptance does not establish email delivery; client confirms receipt.
- Source history supports code rollback, not backups of inboxes, form submissions,
  DNS or provider-account data, and not guaranteed restoration timing.

Email cancellation received before renewal stops care at the paid-period end;
receipt time counts, not when the message is read. Ordinary mid-cycle cancellation
is not prorated, subject to mandatory requirements or failure to provide service.
Refund mistaken renewal charges after timely cancellation. Failed payment receives

access handoff/removal of management access occur within five business days after

## Ownership, cancellation and extras

The client retains supplied materials and gets the paid completed site/source,
with continuing permission to operate, modify and move it, including with another
developer. Define any assignment of identified custom work in the signed agreement,
limited to rights the provider actually owns and can transfer. The provider retains
the reusable base template/general methods. Incorporated provider components receive
a continuing nonexclusive finished-site license; no exclusive base-template sale or
standalone proprietary-template resale is included. Preserve third-party licenses.
Client-owned accounts, domain and fully paid site are not withheld or intentionally
disabled when care ends. Provider availability/renewals remain the client's responsibility.

Build payments are advances, not automatically non-refundable. Before work starts,
refund unearned funds less explicitly approved nonrecoverable third-party costs
where permitted. After work starts, settle documented completed base work at
$75/hour capped at the base build price. Settle separately approved change orders
and third-party costs under their written terms; refund remaining unearned funds.
Provide paid-for deliverables and corresponding rights under the settlement.

Extras: **$75/hour, 15-minute billing increments, 30-minute minimum ($37.50) per
separate approved job**, or an agreed fixed quote. Quote and obtain approval first;
stop for approval if the estimate/scope would be exceeded. No charges for correcting
provider delivery mistakes. Domain/email/provider charges, taxes, full copywriting,
logo design, paid licenses, advanced image work, additional content/pages, CMS,
booking/payments, custom apps, ongoing SEO and Google Business Profile services are
not included. No ranking, lead, score, legal-compliance or absolute-security guarantee.
